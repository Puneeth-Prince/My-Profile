Engineering with a business mindset.

I'm a self-taught developer with approximately 5 years of professional experience, working as a Web Software Developer on database-driven enterprise applications.

My work sits in long-lived business systems: implementing workflows against real rules, writing and tuning SQL Server queries, building reports and Excel exports, and tracking down issues that only appear in production. That means C#, ASP.NET and T-SQL daily, plus the HTML, CSS and JavaScript on top, delivered as responsive interfaces for the people who use them.

I prefer solutions that are understandable and maintainable over unnecessarily complicated ones, and I care about how a system performs and holds up once real data and real users hit it. My goal is to grow into a stronger modern software engineer, with deeper skills in backend engineering, system design, cloud technologies, AI engineering and secure application development.
