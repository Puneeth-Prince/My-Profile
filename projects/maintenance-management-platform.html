Maintenance Management Platform

A business application used to manage maintenance-related workflows, operational activities, dashboards and reporting. Names and descriptions are generalised for confidentiality.

  • C#
  • ASP.NET
  • JavaScript
  • SQL Server
  • HTML
  • CSS

Problem

Maintenance teams needed visibility into maintenance-related workflows, operational activities and status without digging through disconnected records.

Solution

Built and maintained dashboards, status tracking and data filtering on top of a SQL Server backend, applying business rules specific to maintenance and service operations.

Engineering

  • Dashboard development
  • Status tracking
  • Data filtering at scale
  • Maintenance-specific business rules
  • Reporting

Result

Gave maintenance teams a clearer, faster view of operational status, reducing time spent tracking work manually.
